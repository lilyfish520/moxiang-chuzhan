## 活动徽章 · 墨香初绽

**用途**：作文活动荣誉徽章 / 奖章设计，可用于证书、海报、文创周边。

**风格方向**：新中式 · 水墨质感 · 印章韵味 · 少年文学气息

---

### 方案 A：水墨花开（温婉典雅）

**画面核心**：圆形徽章，墨色花瓣层层绽放，中心留白处题写活动名。

**英文提示词**：
```
Badge design, circular emblem, Chinese ink wash painting style (水墨风格).
A blooming flower emerging from ink-black petals, layers of petals unfolding like brushstrokes, with elegant gradient from deep ink black to soft warm gray.
The flower center is empty negative space, as if waiting for calligraphy.
Surrounding the flower, subtle gold foil texture rings form a border, like a traditional Chinese seal.
Background: soft rice paper texture (宣纸肌理), faint ink splatter particles scattered at edges.
Color palette: ink black, warm parchment beige, touches of muted gold and vermillion red.
Minimalist, elegant, literary, suitable for a youth essay competition badge.
Square format, clean vector-like lines, high detail.
```

---

### 方案 B：青墨朱印（书卷气·印章风）

**画面核心**：方形印章式徽章，水墨山峦为底，"墨香初绽"如朱砂印文浮现。

**英文提示词**：
```
Badge design, traditional Chinese seal stamp style (印章风格).
A square emblem with rounded corners, resembling a carved stone seal.
Background: misty ink-wash mountain silhouettes fading into soft clouds, layered in shades of charcoal and pale gray.
In the foreground, the characters for an elegant literary name appear as if stamped in warm vermillion red (朱砂红), slightly textured like real seal paste.
A subtle gold border frames the edges, worn and slightly irregular like an antique seal.
Delicate plum blossom petals drift across the bottom corner.
Rice paper texture throughout.
Color palette: vermillion red, ink black, muted gold, parchment cream.
Square format, elegant, scholarly, competition badge aesthetic.
```

---

### 方案 C：绽放之笔（少年朝气）

**画面核心**：一支毛笔尖端绽放出花朵，象征"笔下生花"。

**英文提示词**：
```
Badge design, circular emblem, modern Chinese illustration style.
A traditional calligraphy brush (毛笔) standing vertically, its tip blossoming into delicate petals and leaves, symbolizing "flowers bloom from the tip of the pen" (妙笔生花).
The petals transition from ink-black at the base to soft peach and pale pink at the tips.
Behind the brush, a faint golden circular seal impression provides depth.
Scattered ink drops transform into tiny stars and flower buds around the edges.
Warm cream background with subtle paper fiber texture.
A thin gold ring borders the emblem.
Color palette: ink black, warm peach pink, soft gold, cream white.
Modern yet elegant, youthful energy, suitable for a student writing competition.
Square format, clean modern linework with traditional ink-wash accents.
```

---

### 方案 D：书窗初绽（极简文艺）

**画面核心**：花窗框景，一枝梅花探入窗内，窗外墨色晕染。

**英文提示词**：
```
Badge design, circular emblem, minimalist Chinese aesthetics.
A traditional garden lattice window (花窗) in the shape of a circle, its wooden frame forming elegant geometric patterns.
Through the window, a single plum blossom branch reaches inward, one bud just beginning to open — capturing the moment of "first bloom" (初绽).
Beyond the window, soft ink-wash gradients suggest a misty scholarly garden.
The window frame is dark wood-brown, the blossom petals are pale pink with vermillion-red stamens.
Negative space predominates, creating a clean modern feel.
Background: pure warm white with the faintest paper grain.
No text in the image — simplicity and suggestion over statement.
Color palette: dark wood brown, pale pink, vermillion accent, warm white.
Square format, extremely clean, literary magazine quality.
```
